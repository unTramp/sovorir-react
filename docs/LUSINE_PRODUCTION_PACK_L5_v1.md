## 1. Production goal

Lesson 5 must feel different from the café Mission in Lesson 4.

The learner should discover a reusable spoken pattern from examples:

- positive: `[thing] + եմ ուզում`;
- negative: `[thing] + չեմ ուզում`.

Media should make the contrast audible and memorable. This lesson does **not** require scene video for MVP. Clear phrase audio is the primary medium.

This is intentional curriculum variation: some lessons benefit from environmental video; some benefit more from tight audio comparison and active production.

## 4. Optional video circle — positive vs negative

**Priority:** MEDIUM  
**Asset:** `l5_circle_want_contrast_v1.mp4`  
**Presentation:** `circle`  
**Duration:** 10–15 sec

### Brief

> Look into camera. Say `Ես թեյ եմ ուզում։` naturally. Brief pause. Then make a subtle rejecting gesture and say `Ես թեյ չեմ ուզում։` naturally. If needed, add only one short Russian bridge: «Хочу / не хочу». Do not explain grammar terminology.

The body cue should support meaning without becoming theatrical.

This clip is optional for publishing because the lesson's core learning mechanism already works through audio + pattern discovery.

---

## 5. No required scene video

Do not create a café or lifestyle scene merely because Lesson 4 used one.

Lesson 5 is deliberately more abstract and generative. Adding a large scene could distract from noticing the structural contrast.

A future review or Mission may reuse these patterns in a scene, but the core Pattern Builder should stay focused.

---

## 6. Pronunciation / rhythm notes for app metadata

Lusine should flag any useful learner-facing note after recording, especially around:

- natural rhythm of `եմ ուզում`;
- natural rhythm of `չեմ ուզում`;
- whether the pronoun `Ես` can be reduced or omitted in casual speech in later lessons;
- whether the selected noun + pattern examples sound neutral and broadly useful.

Do not add advanced omission rules to Lesson 5 unless needed. The MVP goal is a stable beginner pattern.

---

## 7. Native QA checklist

Before approval:

1. Does `Ես ջուր եմ ուզում` sound natural as a beginner-safe sentence?
2. Does `Ես թեյ եմ ուզում` sound natural?
3. Does `Ես սուրճ չեմ ուզում` express the intended simple refusal naturally?
4. Does `Ես թեյ չեմ ուզում` sound natural?
5. Is the positive/negative contrast audible without exaggerated teacher speech?
6. Is the word order appropriate for the intended meaning?
7. Would Lusine recommend a more natural everyday alternative for any of these contexts?

Any wording change that alters the learner-facing canonical sentence must go through curriculum revision before media attachment.
