## 1. Goal

This pack lets Lusine record the human layer of the first three lessons **without designing the lesson architecture herself**.

Sovorir provides:

- exact learning objective;
- exact Armenian line or semantic intent;
- register/context;
- requested take type;
- duration target;
- asset naming;
- framing and delivery notes.

Lusine provides:

- natural Eastern Armenian pronunciation;
- native intonation;
- mouth/articulation reference;
- natural register;
- cultural correction if a scripted line feels artificial;
- on-camera warmth and personality.

Any wording correction by Lusine must be fed back into curriculum QA before final publication.

---

## 2. Recording principles

### Audio

Record clean, dry voice with no music.

For canonical LearningItems:

1. **NORMAL** — natural, clear speech; not exaggeratedly slow.
2. **SLOW** — optional teaching take for phrases where articulation benefits from it.

Leave approximately 2 seconds of silence between takes during batch recording.

### Video circles

- portrait framing;
- face and mouth clearly visible;
- simple neutral/warm background;
- look into lens;
- one teaching idea per clip;
- no long introduction;
- target 4–20 sec;
- do not bake large captions into the video; captions/transcript belong to app metadata.

### Scene videos

- target 8–35 sec;
- prioritize believable interaction over acting;
- learner should infer intent from context;
- shoot enough headroom/crop safety for mobile;
- when possible record isolated audio as well.

### Lesson videos

Use only when a concept actually benefits from an explanation. Target 30–90 sec. Lessons 1–3 do not require a long lesson video for MVP.

- `Բարև ձեզ` should preserve natural politeness without theatrical formality.

## 3.3 Video circle — goodbye articulation

**Asset:** `l1_circle_stesityun_v1.mp4`  
**Duration:** 7–10 sec

**Brief:**

# 4. Lesson 2 — Introductions

**Primary archetype:** Discovery + Conversation Builder  
**Human role:** make introductions feel social, not like four vocabulary cards.

## 4.1 Phrase audio batch

| Asset key | Armenian | Meaning | Register | Takes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `l2_anuns_name_e` | `Անունս Անդրեյ է` | Меня зовут Андрей | neutral | NORMAL + pattern take |
| `l2_inch_e_qo_anuny` | `Ի՞նչ է քո անունը` | Как тебя зовут? | informal | NORMAL + SLOW |
| `l2_inch_e_dzer_anuny` | `Ի՞նչ է ձեր անունը` | Как вас зовут? | polite | NORMAL + SLOW |
| `l2_shat_hajeli_e` | `Շատ հաճելի է` | Очень приятно | neutral | NORMAL |

### Pattern take

For the name construction additionally record:

- `Անունս Անի է։`
- `Անունս Լուսինե է։`

These demonstrate that the learner replaces only the name slot.

## 4.2 Video circle — discovery opener

**Asset:** `l2_circle_anuns_ani_e_v1.mp4`  
**Duration:** 5–8 sec  
**Purpose:** input before translation is revealed.

**Exact Armenian:**

> `Բարև։ Անունս Անի է։`

For final Lusine version use:

> `Բարև։ Անունս Լուսինե է։`

**Direction:**

Natural first-meeting smile. Gesture lightly toward yourself on the name phrase. No Russian translation. The learner must infer intent from context.

## 4.3 Video circle — informal vs polite name question

**Asset:** `l2_circle_name_register_v1.mp4`  
**Duration:** 10–14 sec

**Brief:**

> Say the informal question as if speaking to a peer: `Ի՞նչ է քո անունը։` Then say the polite form as if meeting an older/unfamiliar person: `Ի՞նչ է ձեր անունը։` Keep the contrast in tone subtle and natural. End there.

## 4.4 Scene video — first meeting

**Asset:** `l2_scene_language_club_v1.mp4`  
**Duration:** 18–25 sec  
**Purpose:** full conversation input and later review reuse.

Suggested dialogue:

- A: `Բարև։ Անունս Նարեկ է։`
- B: `Բարև։ Անունս Լուսինե է։`
- A: `Շատ հաճելի է։`
- B: `Ինձ նույնպես։`

The learner is not expected to learn `Ինձ նույնպես` yet. It is comprehensible scene input, not a required LearningItem.

## 4.5 Batch QA questions for Lusine

Before approving the lesson:

- Does `Անունս … է` sound like a natural beginner-friendly choice in these contexts?
- Is the informal/polite contrast presented naturally?
- Would a native speaker phrase either question differently in the scripted situation?
- Is `Շատ հաճելի է` a natural response in the shown scene?

## 5.2 Required connected-speech chunks

Record as whole lines, not stitched phrase assets:

**Chunk A**

> `Լավ եմ, շնորհակալություն։ Իսկ դո՞ւ։`

**Chunk B**

> `Լավ եմ, շնորհակալություն։ Իսկ դո՞ւք։`

**Mini-dialogue informal**

> A: `Բարև։ Ո՞նց ես։`  
> B: `Լավ եմ, շնորհակալություն։ Իսկ դո՞ւ։`  
> A: `Ես էլ լավ եմ։`

The lesson may expose `Ես էլ լավ եմ` without adding it to the required item set yet.

## 5.4 Video circle — articulation / shadowing

**Brief:**

# 8. Production asset states

Every planned asset should eventually have one of:

- `planned` — brief exists, no recording;
- `recorded` — raw take exists;
- `selected` — approved take chosen;
- `processed` — edited/normalized/encoded;
- `attached` — linked to lesson metadata;
- `qa-approved` — native + product QA passed;
- `published`.

The lesson architecture must remain usable with fake media while assets are `planned`.
